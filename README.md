# Summitbound v0.14.0 — Vertical Precision & Fair Range

**Playable:** one reviewed five-peak course. **Campaign goal:** exactly 100 quality-controlled levels (not yet implemented).

The user's v0.13 playtest exposed two real problems: a jump could bypass the intended ledges and climbs, and the timing spectrum did not offer a near-vertical recovery/control option.

**Changes**
- **Intentional near-vertical release:** during roughly the first 20–22% of the pole ride, fully loaded recoil can generate a large vertical rise with effectively zero horizontal launch speed. The HUD and vault meter explicitly label this phase **VERTICAL**.
- **Balanced distance:** the middle of the ride still rewards approach momentum; the full-speed same-height flight reaches roughly 877–928 world units rather than the former ~1319. Beyond ~66% of the ride energy progressively dissipates; overholding loses most of the range. Deterministic, no invisible landing assist or horizontal hard clamp.
- **Summit geometry correction:** widen actual rock underneath ledges without extending the **walkable snowy summit collider** leftwards. This previously turned a structural rock fix into a shortcut over all the landing shelves. The physical ice exits remain connected to their actual summits.
- **Proof:** simulated full-speed flights at ~20%, 55%, 85% release gain ~550, 408, 169 world units of height and ~29, 877, 553 units of same-height horizontal range respectively. At the fourth checkpoint's actual geography, full real-physics landing samples still offer multiple safe windows. An independent first-contact sweep across all four crossings found **no direct summit-to-summit shortcuts** in the reviewed Five Peaks slice.
- **Practice:** same-peak retries remain available for learning; Hardcore/Time Trial remain punishing. PB/ghost data version 7 prevents comparing incompatible physics and terrain records.
- **Airborne control:** steering authority reduced from 225 to 90. It still permits recoveries and positioning after a nearly vertical jump, but cannot accumulate enough sideways momentum to bypass an entire mountain. The direct-summit first-contact tests now also check full left/neutral/right steering across all four crossings.
- **Quality gate:** `npm run test:precision-vault` is wired into CI and checks vertical control, maximum free-flight range, timing differences, momentum dependence, zero direct-summit bypass, five-peak course graph and both real campsite checkpoint ledges. The previously approved 997-seed reference layouts remain archived.

**Caveats:** desktop and Android hands-on playtests are still required; simulated geometrical reachability does not certify enjoyable level difficulty, artwork quality or timing medals. Wind, future airborne axe saves and the 100-level content engine remain future phases.

# Summitbound v0.13.0 — Dynamic Pole Vault Rebuild

**One playable five-peak vertical slice; exactly 100 levels remains the eventual campaign target.** The former gameplay is preserved in `backup/pre-vault-dynamics-v0121`.

**Problem actually identified:** the former vault combined a largely scripted pole motion with a separate release impulse. Much of the pole's visible bend was cosmetic, and airborne horizontal velocity was capped at 650 each frame, masking stronger releases. Increasing top sprint speed alone could never solve the missing distinction between high versus far vaults.

**Implemented:**
- `public/game/scripts/pole-dynamics.js` defines the physical swing and spring-unloading release model, loaded after the authored Five Peaks course but before the game state. The player's hand positions move continuously around a planted anchor, with pole length constrained to 195 world units. Compression and release progress are tied to the same swing timeline.
- **Meaningful timing:** early release produces a high arc; a mid-swing release reaches substantially farther with less height; waiting too long bleeds energy. Run-up speed affects BOTH the achieved height and range, with no random wobble or invisible rescue.
- **Actual flight unchanged by the old cap:** in-air velocity clipping is now 1050 rather than 650, and the new course validation uses the actual release/flight calculations.
- **Fixed course layout:** the five-peak mountain geometry is frozen in `FEATURED_BASE_LAYOUTS` instead of silently being regenerated by changing the pole's physics. The old archived procedural routes use the unchanged legacy sampler.
- **HUD:** meter labels explain high, long and overheld release phases; the in-world status displays the phase and percentage of initial run-up; old misleading single 'gold release window' decoration removed.
- **Personal bests:** previous trial ghosts do not carry over to incompatible physics (record format v6). Medal targets remain provisional; real playthrough times and feedback must set appropriate thresholds.
- **Regression:** `npm run test:vault-dynamics`, CI-enabled, checks complete projectile range and peak height across a release timing sweep, real sprint effects, fixed pole length, smooth swings, non-clipping gameplay release, five-peak reachability and archived physics compatibility.

**Illustrative measured flight from the first takeoff at full run-up:** at a release 25% through the pole ride, height above takeoff ≈626 and same-height range ≈895 game units; at 55%, height ≈440 and range ≈1319; at 85%, height ≈196 and range ≈1030; at 115%, range collapses to ≈335. These are free-flight reference trajectories, not guaranteed real-course landing points. Real success still depends on terrain intersection, braking room and precise release timing.

**Still open:** actual browser/mobile user feel, a cohesive rugged art overhaul, real Android profiling, stricter level-difficulty calibration and a generative architecture that can produce 100 genuinely excellent individual levels. Do not interpret automated numerical validation as full gameplay sign-off.

- **Qualification:** the Four-Crossing Five Peaks course remains physics-validated with available 300/375/450-speed samples. - **Tests:** `npm run test:peak-retry`, wired into CI, covers true peak respawn, campsite preservation, stricter modes, launch velocity scaling, the course audit and finite ledge runways. A physical desktop/Android feel review and full CI result remain outstanding.

**Next player check:** use Practice and the Retry Peak button to repeatedly attempt the difficult campsite leap. Compare low-speed and full-speed vaults and test the release timing. Our long-term principle remains difficult but repeatable leaps, fair checkpoints, and no random movement or catch assistance.

# Summitbound v0.10.0 — Phase 8 Skill Foundation (gameplay rebuild)

**Implementation checkpoint, awaiting real playtest and complete CI.** Active work stays on `main` for Google AI Studio; backup `backup/pre-phase8-v0924` retains the old implementation.
